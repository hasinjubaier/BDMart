# Domain Glossary

This glossary establishes the definitive technical and commercial terminology for BD Mart.

| Term | Domain | Definition & Technical Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **Poisha (পয়সা)** | Financials | The minor currency unit of Bangladesh (1 BDT = 100 poisha). All monetary amounts in database tables (`subtotal`, `delivery_fee`, `vat`, `total`, `amount`) are stored as 64-bit integers (`bigint` or `integer`) to eliminate floating-point rounding errors. |
| **Taka (৳ / BDT)** | Financials | The primary currency of Bangladesh. Formatted for display using the standard `৳` prefix without extra spacing (e.g., `৳1,450`), rendered in Bengali or English digits based on user locale. |
| **Escrow Ledger** | Financials | An intermediary financial buffer (`escrow_ledger` table). Customer payments are held in `held` state by the platform and released to the seller (`released`) only after verifiable delivery confirmation from the courier. |
| **MOQ** | Commerce (B2B) | **Minimum Order Quantity**. The minimum unit count a business buyer must purchase in a single order line to qualify for wholesale purchasing. Enforced strictly at server level. |
| **Price Tier** | Commerce (B2B) | Volume-dependent unit pricing (`price_tiers` table) where unit cost decreases as volume tiers (`min_qty`) increase. Visible exclusively to verified business buyers. |
| **RFQ** | Commerce (B2B) | **Request for Quotation**. A formal commercial inquiry issued by a business buyer specifying custom volume, technical specifications, and target pricing. |
| **Quote** | Commerce (B2B) | A formal binding offer submitted by a seller in response to an RFQ (`rfq_quotes` table), specifying unit price, validity period, and delivery lead days. |
| **TIN** | Compliance | **Taxpayer Identification Number**. A mandatory 12-digit tax registration number issued by the National Board of Revenue (NBR) in Bangladesh, required for business buyer and seller onboarding. |
| **BIN** | Compliance | **Business Identification Number**. A 9-digit or 13-digit Value Added Tax (VAT) registration number issued by NBR, mandatory for issuing legal Mushak 6.3 tax invoices. |
| **Trade License** | Compliance | Municipal business operating permit issued by City Corporations or Union Parishads in Bangladesh. Mandatory for seller KYC verification. |
| **COD** | Logistics / Payment | **Cash on Delivery**. Physical cash collection by the courier delivery agent upon physical delivery to the buyer. |
| **IPN** | Payments | **Instant Payment Notification**. Server-to-server webhook callback transmitted by payment gateways (SSLCommerz/aamarPay) to confirm transaction authorization. |
| **Idempotency Key** | API & Data | A unique UUID transmitted in webhooks and mutation headers to prevent duplicate execution of payments, orders, or stock allocations upon network retries. |
| **3PL** | Logistics | **Third-Party Logistics**. Independent delivery courier networks (e.g., Pathao Courier, Steadfast Courier) integrated via API adapters. |
| **Consignment / Tracking No** | Logistics | Unique shipment identifier generated by the courier API and stored in `shipments.tracking_no`. |
| **Digital Commerce Guidelines (2021)** | Legal / Regulatory | National operational framework enacted by Bangladesh Ministry of Commerce mandating delivery timelines, price transparency, escrow protection, and prohibition of deceptive commercial practices. |
